Hover-reactive card. Tracks the pointer over its surface, projects the cursor onto a conic-gradient mask, and reveals the mesh-gradient border + outer halo only on the side closest to the cursor. The optional `animated` flag plays a one-shot sweep on mount so the effect advertises itself before any hover.

## Mechanism

- A pointermove listener on the card writes two CSS variables: `--gc-angle` (cursor angle, 0–360deg) and proximity-derived `--gc-border-opacity` / `--gc-glow-opacity`.
- The border layer is a stack of `radial-gradient` mesh slices used as a `border-box` background, masked by a `conic-gradient` aimed at the cursor — so only the side facing the pointer shows the gradient.
- The fill layer is the same mesh palette painted into the padding box, masked through a multi-step composite (subtract, add×5) so the fill bleeds inward from the corners and from the cursor side, blended `soft-light` over the card background.
- The outer halo is a `box-shadow` stack on a sibling element, also masked by a conic gradient so the halo is asymmetric — concentrated on the cursor's side.

## Notes

- **Requires a dark surface.** The default `backgroundColor` is dark; the mesh + halo are designed to read on dark. Use the `data-theme="dark"` wrapper or a custom dark background — see the [Layout](/components/layout/) docs for page-wide and per-section dark themes.
- **No tokens consumed.** The component takes its colors entirely from props — won't drift across themes.
- The sweep animation runs once on mount. To replay it from JS, dispatch a synthetic `pointerenter` then `pointerleave`, or re-mount the component (`<GlowCard key=…>`-style if you wrap it).
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables the cross-fade transitions but the cursor-tracked variables still update; the change is just instant. The mount sweep is also visually muted because the transitions are zero.
