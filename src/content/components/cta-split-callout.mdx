## Notes

- **Composition.** Built from `SectionMain` (page rhythm + diagonal pattern columns) and `Button` (primary variant). No new design tokens introduced.
- **Bullet count.** Three to five reads best. Past six the column gets taller than the text column and the layout starts to lean.
- **Use when.** Conversion pages where the user already trusts the value but needs a checklist nudge. Not the right fit for cold traffic — that wants the image-driven CTAs.
