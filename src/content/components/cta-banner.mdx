## Notes

- **Theming.** The inner panel is wrapped in `data-theme="dark"`, so it reads as inverted whether the page is light or dark. Tokens like `bg-canvas`/`text-fg` resolve through the dark theme inside that wrapper.
- **No image.** Banner is intentionally pattern-free — it's the calmest of the four CTAs and reads as "the call" rather than "the pitch."
- **Padding.** Inner panel uses `contentPadding="none"` on `SectionMain` so the dark rectangle goes edge-to-edge of the content column. Internal padding is on the panel itself.
